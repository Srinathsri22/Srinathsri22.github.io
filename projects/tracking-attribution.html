The Problem

Declining pixel quality on a $1M+ spend pixel

Snapchat campaign performance was declining due to a Poor Event Quality Score (EQS) on a pixel with $1M+ in spend history. Snapchat attributed it to low scCid append rate, but the actual root cause was different.

On Meta, iOS tracking restrictions and browser-side signal degradation were eroding attribution accuracy. Call campaigns had attribution gaps from postback timing.

What I Built

Snapchat CAPI Gateway — Root Cause Diagnosis

Through analysis of raw CAPI payloads from Snapchat's event logs (Elasticsearch), I identified the real root cause:

  • RedTrack was correctly capturing and transmitting scCid
  • But the em (hashed email) and ph (hashed phone) fields were consistently empty in every payload
  • This missing first-party data was the actual EQS driver, not scCid
  • Additional issues: empty event_source_url, event_time in milliseconds instead of Unix seconds, potential event_id reuse
Key Insight

The root cause was found in raw payload analysis — not where Snapchat pointed. Their support team attributed the issue to scCid append rate, but the data showed scCid was fine. Missing PII fields were the actual problem.

Custom CAPI Gateway Architecture

Designed a multi-vertical, multi-pixel CAPI gateway to bypass RedTrack's PII forwarding limitation:

  • Receive Everflow postbacks carrying scCid as a sub-parameter passed through RedTrack
  • Enrich with SHA-256 hashed email and phone from the client
  • Fire corrected CAPI payloads directly to Snapchat
  • Full event dashboard for monitoring fired postbacks and event quality

New clean pixel (MEDVI_CAPI_V2) planned for new campaigns. Existing campaigns left untouched. Customer List seeding via hashed PII also planned for Lookalike audience building.

Meta Server-Side CAPI

Implemented on all O&O properties, bypassing iOS tracking restrictions and browser-side signal degradation entirely.

  • 9+ Event Quality Score maintained across all active verticals
  • Custom postback and Meta CAPI integration on RemodelWell sending first-party conversion data and revenue signals directly to Meta

Call Campaign Attribution

Configured granular 2-minute and 5-minute postback intervals in Ringba for faster and more accurate lead attribution on call-based campaigns. Resolved postback tracking issues and eliminated revenue leakage from misattributed conversions.

Buyer Onboarding & Operational Standard

  • 6 live buyers onboarded with end-to-end lead routing, postback verification, and ZIP code coverage mapping
  • All buyer-reported rejection issues resolved through structured analysis and coordinated fixes
  • Proactive postback verification established as a team standard: sending and verifying postback links with clients before every new offer delivery
  • Zero postback failures recorded since adoption
