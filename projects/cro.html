The Problem

Underperforming landing page CTR

Landing page click-through rates were underperforming, directly impacting top-of-funnel efficiency and downstream CPL across all active verticals.

What I Built

Structured CRO Initiative

Optimized landing page copy, layout, CTA placement, and load speed based on funnel analysis across multiple affiliate verticals.

Measurement Approach

Performance measured over two consecutive 30-day windows:

Baseline

Mar 16 — Apr 16

Pre-optimization performance baseline across all verticals

Post-delivery

Apr 17 — May 17

Performance measured after CRO changes shipped

Results

  • LPCTR improved from 8.72% to 10.22%
  • +1.5 percentage point absolute gain
  • +17.2% relative uplift within 30 days of delivery
Highlight

A separate blur-effect lander variant for the Medvi health vertical recorded 26.44% LPCTR over a 30-day window (Apr 24 to May 23), validating the creative format and establishing a new performance benchmark for the vertical.
